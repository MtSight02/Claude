# Lark & Luce · 04 品牌语气与文案

> 所有对外文案用英文（美国拼写为主；欧盟英文市场同样适用，必要时 colour/color 不强求统一）。中文仅作为内部备注。红线复核：只写 316L stainless steel / cubic zirconia (CZ) / water-resistant / tarnish-resistant；不写绝对化低敏、医疗暗示、永久防水、永不褪色、无依据环保声明。

## 1. 语气原则

**一句话：像清晨对朋友轻声说话——短句、具体、带一点诗意，然后停下。**

| 原则 | 说明 | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| **安静（Hushed）** | 不喊、不用全大写、不堆感叹号 | "A small light for ordinary mornings." | "OMG SO SPARKLY!!! You NEED these!!!" |
| **具体（Precise）** | 用数字和材料替代形容词 | "6mm, fireworks-cut, 316L steel." | "Amazing premium luxury quality!" |
| **诗意但克制** | 每段最多一个比喻 | "Cut to scatter first light." | "Like a thousand stars dancing in a moonlit ocean of dreams." |
| **诚实** | 讲清楚是什么、不是什么 | "Cubic zirconia — cut with the care usually saved for fine stones." | 暗示是宝石、贵金属或"同等品质" |
| **温暖（不冷漠）** | 用 "you"，偶尔用 "we" | "Wear them to the café, the meeting, the late train home." | "Product is suitable for daily use." |
| **欧洲感** | 偶尔用意大利/法语小词，但必须一看就懂 | "*Luce* — light, in Italian." | 整段外语、生僻词 |

### 改写示例（Before → After）

| 场景 | Before（不合格） | After（Lark & Luce） | 中文备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 广告标题 | "Insane bling for only $22!! Limited stock!!" | "First light, $22. Yours to keep." | 去掉焦虑感与感叹号 |
| 材质 | "Premium metal, safe for all ears" | "316L stainless steel — the same steel family used in many body-jewelry pieces." | 不做绝对化承诺 |
| 防水 | "Shower, swim, sleep — they'll never change!" | "Water-resistant and made for everyday wear. Take them off for chlorine and salt water." | 不写永久 |
| 促销 | "BIGGEST SALE EVER 50% OFF EVERYTHING" | "A quiet thank-you: 15% off, this weekend only." | 折扣也要安静 |
| 社媒 caption | "New drop!!! Run don't walk" | "New this month: the Blue Hour stud. For the minutes after sunset." | 节奏慢 |
| 客服 | "Unfortunately we cannot process that." | "Let me sort this for you — here's what we can do." | 先解决再解释 |

**FAQ**

1. **What is a fireworks cut?**
   A round cut with many small facets radiating from the center, like a burst of fireworks. It breaks light into finer, busier flashes than a standard round cut — which is why a 6mm stone looks so lively. *(See the facet diagram above.)*

2. **Can I wear them in the shower?**
   316L steel and our PVD coating are made for everyday wear and are water-resistant. Everyday splashes and showers are fine for most people, but no coating lasts indefinitely: chlorine, salt water, and harsh soaps will shorten its life. Take them off for the pool and the sea.

3. **Are they suitable for sensitive ears?**
   Our studs are 316L stainless steel, the same steel family used in many body-jewelry pieces. Like all stainless steel it contains nickel, bound in the alloy. [After report received: Every batch is nickel-release tested to EU REACH limits.] If you have a known nickel allergy, please check with your doctor before wearing.

4. **What does "AAAAA-grade" mean?**
   It's a grading term used in the CZ industry for the clearest, most precisely cut stones. It's not a gemological certification — it's our promise that we buy the top grade our cutters make.

5. **Can I return them?**
   Yes — within 30 days. For hygiene reasons, earrings must be unworn and in their original sealed packaging. If anything arrives damaged, write to us and we'll make it right. [EU withdrawal-right wording to be confirmed with counsel.]
