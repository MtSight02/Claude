## 2. 写作规则

### 2.1 Do

1. **短句。** 每句 ≤ 12 个词为佳，像咖啡馆黑板上的字。
2. **一个包袱原则**：每条内容最多一个 wink / wren 双关。
3. **具体胜过形容词**：写 "6mm fireworks-cut CZ" 而不是 "stunning sparkly gem"。
4. **对话感**：用 you / we，偶尔用括号做"悄悄话"（like this）。
5. **诚实是最好的调情**：材质、镀层、检测状态大方写出来。
6. **法式点缀，适可而止**：每页最多一个法语词（*voilà*, *encore*, *petit*），用斜体。

### 2.2 Don't

1. 不用 OMG、babe、bestie、cutie、slay、obsessed 堆叠（单独偶尔一次可以）。
2. 每条 emoji ≤ 1 个，只用 ✦ 或 🍒；**品牌账号永不用 😉**。
3. 不全大写喊叫，不用 "!!!"。
4. 不制造焦虑：不写 "Don't be the only one without…"。
5. 不碰红线词：材质只写 316L stainless steel；宝石只写 cubic zirconia / CZ；不写绝对化低敏、永久防水、永不变色；不写未经证明的环保说法。
6. 不写"surgical"等医疗暗示。

| "Waterproof, never tarnishes, wear it forever!" | "Water-resistant and tarnish-resistant. Made for everyday wear." | 红线 5 |
| "Perfect for sensitive ears — totally safe!" | "316L stainless steel — the same steel family used in many body-jewelry pieces." | 红线 2 |

**FAQ:**

**1. What makes a fireworks cut different?**
A standard round stone has a classic facet pattern. Our fireworks cut adds many smaller facets radiating from the center, so the light breaks into tinier, sharper flashes. In plain terms: more little sparkles, more often. (See the facet diagram above.)

**2. Is this okay for sensitive ears?**
Our studs are 316L stainless steel — the same steel family used in many body-jewelry pieces. 316L does contain some nickel, though it's bound tightly in the alloy. If you have a known nickel allergy, please check with your doctor before wearing any metal jewelry. [After testing: "Every production batch is nickel-release tested to EU REACH limits."]

**3. Can I shower or work out in it?**
It's water-resistant and tarnish-resistant, made for everyday wear. For the longest life, take it off for swimming, hot tubs and saunas, and keep it away from harsh chemicals.

**4. Is the stone real?**
It's cubic zirconia — a lab-created stone prized for its clarity and brightness. We use our highest grade (AAAAA) and cut every stone in our fireworks cut. We'll never call it anything it isn't.

**5. What's your return policy?**
30 days to return or exchange unworn earrings in their original sealed packaging. For hygiene reasons we can't accept earrings that have been worn. EU customers keep their statutory 14-day right of withdrawal. Full details on our Returns page.
