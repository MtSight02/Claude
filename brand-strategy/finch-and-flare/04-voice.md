## 1. 语气准则

### 1.1 一句话

**像一个懂珠宝、有点幽默、从不推销的朋友，用杂志编辑的笔法说话。**

### 1.2 语气刻度（1–10）

| 维度 | 刻度 | 说明 |
|---|---:|---|
| 正式 ↔ 随意 | 6 | 比奢侈品随意，比快时尚克制 |
| 严肃 ↔ 幽默 | 4 | 幽默是"一句话里的一个小转折"，不是段子 |
| 克制 ↔ 热情 | 4 | 少用感叹号（全站每页 ≤1 个） |
| 专业 ↔ 通俗 | 5 | 讲切工、316L 时说清楚，但不堆术语 |

### 1.3 写作规则

1. **短句优先**。标题 ≤ 8 个词。
2. **具体胜过形容词**：写 "6mm, 57 facets [待供应商确认刻面数]" 而不是 "stunning, luxurious"。
3. **一个画面一个笑点**，最多。
4. **主语是"你"和"光"**，少说 "we are passionate about…"。
5. **诚实的限定词是品牌资产**：写 "tarnish-resistant"、"made for everyday wear"，并说明怎么保养。
6. **禁用词表**：luxury / luxurious、stunning、must-have、OMG、babe、bestie、queen、"limited time only!!!"、以及红线中的所有绝对化材质与功效说法。
7. 美式拼写为主站默认（color, jewelry）；欧盟站英文同样用美式拼写保持统一，德/法/荷语版本由母语译者本地化，不机翻上线。

### 1.4 Do / Don't 改写示例

| 场景 | ❌ Don't | ✅ Do | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 产品标题 | "OMG Sparkly Luxury Stud Earrings!!" | "The Flare — 6mm Fireworks-Cut Studs" | 具体、自信 |
| 材质说明 | "Premium metal that never fades and is safe for all ears" | "316L stainless steel with an 18K gold-tone PVD finish. Tarnish-resistant and made for everyday wear." | 不绝对化，给事实 |
| 价格 | "Unbelievable price, only $22!!!" | "$22. Yes, really. Spend the rest on the matching pair." | 自信 + 一点幽默 |
| 促销 | "HUGE SALE 50% OFF EVERYTHING HURRY" | "Once a year, we dim the prices. 20% off, through Monday." | 有节制，保护价格锚 |
| 锆石描述 | 把 CZ 比作贵宝石 | "AAAAA-grade cubic zirconia — our highest-clarity, most precisely cut CZ grade." | 红线第 3、4 条 |
| 防水 | "Wear it in the ocean forever!" | "Water-resistant and made for everyday wear. Take them off for the pool — chlorine is nobody's friend." | 红线第 5 条 + 幽默 |
| 社媒评论回复 | "Thank u babe!! 💕💕" | "That's exactly the light we were hoping for. Thank you, Sam." | 温暖但不甜腻 |
| 缺货 | "Sold out sorry!" | "Gone in a flash. Join the list and we'll tell you first." | 品牌化 |
| 错误页 404 | "Page not found." | "This page flew off. Let's get you back to the light." | 呼应 finch |

### 3.5 Care

> **Keep the fireworks going.**
> - Put them on last, after lotion, perfume and hairspray.
> - Water-resistant and made for everyday wear — but take them off for the pool, the hot tub and the sea. Chlorine and salt are hard on every finish.
> - Wipe with your pouch (yes, it's also a polishing cloth) to bring back the sparkle.
> - Store them in their matchbox so they don't scratch their neighbors.

### 3.6 FAQ（5 条）

**1. Is this real gold?**
No — and we'd rather tell you than let you guess. The gold-tone version is 316L stainless steel with an 18K gold-tone PVD finish. PVD bonds the color to the steel, which makes it more durable than standard plating. It's tarnish-resistant and made for everyday wear.

**2. Is it a real gemstone?**
It's cubic zirconia (CZ), a lab-made stone known for its clarity and sparkle. We use our highest grade, which we call AAAAA — our highest-clarity, most precisely cut CZ grade. The fireworks cut is what makes it look the way it does.

**3. I have sensitive ears. Will these work for me?**
They're made from 316L stainless steel, the same steel family used in many body-jewelry pieces. 316L does contain some nickel, but it's bound tightly in the steel, and release is typically very low. [仅在拿到报告后启用：Every batch is tested to EU nickel-release limits (EN 1811).] If you have a known nickel allergy, please check with your doctor before wearing any new jewelry.

**4. Can I shower and sleep in them?**
They're water-resistant and made for everyday wear, so a quick shower is fine. We'd still take them off for swimming, the gym and sleep — studs last longer (and so do earlobes) with a little rest. Dry them after contact with water.

**5. What's your return policy?**
30 days from delivery. For hygiene reasons, earrings need to be unworn and in their original packaging with the seal intact [欧盟卫生例外适用性需律师确认]. If something isn't right with your pair, email us — a real person named [客服署名] will answer within one business day.

> **About Finch & Flare**
>
> ### Small bird. Big spark.
>
> A finch is a small, quick, bright bird. You don't always see it arrive. You just notice that the windowsill looks better than it did a second ago.
>
> A flare is a burst of light. Brief, bright, impossible to ignore.
>
> We put the two together because that's what we wanted our jewelry to do: be small, be quick to put on, and catch the light in a way that makes people look twice.
>
> ### Why the fireworks cut
>
> Most everyday studs shine. We wanted ours to *spark*. So we built the collection around one cut — a round stone with dozens of facets radiating from the center — that breaks light into tiny scattered flashes, like a firework seen from far away. Every piece we make starts with that cut. It's the thing we obsess over so you don't have to.
